Privacy Policy

First-pass draft, pending legal review against Nigeria's NDPA, Kenya's Data Protection Act, Ghana's Data Protection Act, and South Africa's POPIA. Company details below (shown in brackets) will be filled in before this is treated as final.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email, phone number, password (stored hashed), and account type (Customer, Provider, Admin).
  • Provider verification documents: government-issued ID and/or business registration documents submitted for verification, stored in encrypted cloud storage and accessible only to Admin reviewers.
  • Booking information: services booked, dates/times, booking status, and any notes exchanged about a booking.
  • Payment information: our payment provider processes and stores card/payment details directly — Naa here does not store full card numbers. We retain records of transaction amounts and status for accounting and dispute purposes.
  • Messages: content of direct messages and group chats sent through the Platform's messaging features.
  • Device and usage information: IP address, browser type, and general usage logs, collected automatically for security and diagnostics.

We only collect what's needed to operate bookings, verification, payments, and communication on the Platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to: create and manage your account; connect Customers and Providers for bookings; verify Provider identity/business legitimacy before allowing them to list services; process payments through our payment provider; send booking confirmations, reminders, and account-related notifications; respond to support requests; detect and prevent fraud or abuse; and improve the Platform's reliability and features.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your data for purposes unrelated to operating the Platform without asking you first.

3. Legal Basis and Applicable Law

Naa here operates across Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, and South Africa, each with its own data protection framework — including Nigeria's Data Protection Act (NDPA), Kenya's Data Protection Act, and South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Where these laws require a stated legal basis for processing, we rely on: performance of the contract between you and Naa here (creating your account, processing a booking); your consent (for optional features, such as messaging or marketing communications); and our legitimate interest in operating a safe, functioning marketplace (fraud prevention, verification review).

4. Who We Share Information With

We share information only as needed to run the Platform:

  • Payment provider — to process bookings and hold funds in escrow until service completion.
  • Cloud storage provider (Cloudflare R2) — to store verification documents and listing media securely.
  • Email delivery provider (Resend) — to send account and booking notification emails.
  • Hosting provider — to run the Platform's servers and database.
  • Other users, where relevant to a booking — for example, a Provider sees a Customer's name and booking details needed to deliver the service; Providers' business information and verification status (not the underlying documents) are visible to Customers.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, only where legally required.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Retention and Security

We keep account and booking information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period after closure to meet legal, accounting, and dispute-resolution obligations. Verification documents are retained only as long as needed for Provider verification and compliance, then deleted or anonymized.

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted storage for verification documents, access controls limiting who can view sensitive data (only Admin reviewers can view verification documents), and secure transmission (HTTPS) across the Platform. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users and relevant authorities in the event of a data breach as required by applicable law.

6. Your Rights

Subject to the law of your country, you generally have the right to: access the personal information we hold about you; correct inaccurate information; request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to what we must legally retain); object to certain uses of your data; and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [Contact Email]. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with the requirements of applicable law.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies/local storage needed to keep you logged in and the Platform functioning. We do not currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Children's Privacy

Naa here is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If we learn a minor's account was created, we will close it and delete the associated data.

9. International Data Transfers

Because our hosting and cloud storage infrastructure may be located outside the country where you use the Platform, your information may be processed in a different country than your own. Where this occurs, we take steps to ensure your information continues to receive an appropriate level of protection.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated before they take effect, for example by email or an in-app notice.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests regarding your personal information, can be sent to [Contact Email].
